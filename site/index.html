01 / PET-AWARE

It follows your pet,
not your keyboard.

When the Codex pet disappears, the potions and network refresh disappear with it. Bring the pet back and the HUD returns automatically.

  • Native Swift/AppKit and .NET 8/WPF
  • No keyboard capture. No token logging.
  • The original pet stays centered and clickable.
